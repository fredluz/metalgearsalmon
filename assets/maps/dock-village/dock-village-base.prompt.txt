Deterministic top-down base map generated from src/rooms.js walkBounds.
Ground-only nighttime fishing dock map: water, mud shore, wooden plank piers, mooring outlines, cleats, rope curls, and plank highlights.
No actors, UI, labels, barrels, crates, lantern props, or boat props are baked into this base; those remain transparent prop layers.
Art and collision intentionally share the same authored geometry to avoid walkable-water and blocked-dock mismatch.
